## Retail Sales – Exploratory Data Analysis

## Objective
Explore a retail sales transaction dataset to uncover sales trends, customer demographics, product category performance, and relationships between numerical variables, and turn those findings into actionable business recommendations.

# Dataset
`retail_sales_dataset.csv` — 1,000 transactions, 9 columns (Transaction ID, Date, Customer ID, Gender, Age, Product Category, Quantity, Price per Unit, Total Amount). No missing values, no duplicates.

# Analysis performed
- **Data quality check** — verified completeness, uniqueness of Transaction/Customer IDs, and consistent categorical values.
- **Descriptive statistics** — mean, median, mode, and standard deviation for Age, Quantity, Price per Unit, and Total Amount.
- **Time trends** — monthly and quarterly revenue aggregation and trend charts.
- **Customer segmentation** — sales broken down by age group (18–24 through 65+) and gender.
- **Product category performance** — revenue, quantity sold, and average transaction value by category.
- **Correlation analysis** — heatmap of Age, Quantity, Price per Unit, and Total Amount.

# Key findings
- Total revenue: ₦456,000 across 1,000 transactions (2,514 units sold); average transaction ≈ ₦456.
- May 2023 was the strongest month (≈₦53,150); Q4 2023 was the strongest complete quarter.
- **Electronics** generated the highest revenue; **Clothing** had the highest quantity sold — volume and revenue leaders are not the same category.
- Female customers slightly outsold male customers, though the gap is small.
- Younger customers (18–24) had higher average transaction values than older age groups.
- Price per Unit correlates strongly with Total Amount; Age shows little relationship with spending behavior.
- The dataset has no product-level field, so a "Top 10 products" analysis wasn't possible without inventing data — flagged explicitly rather than fabricated.

# Business recommendations
1. Prioritize inventory and promotions for Electronics (highest revenue category).
2. Use bundling/cross-selling in Clothing to lift its average transaction value, given its high volume but lower revenue.
3. Tailor marketing by age/gender segment based on differing average transaction values.
4. Use pricing and product mix strategically, since price per unit is the strongest driver of transaction value.
